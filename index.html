Dream Invent Inspire

An invention factory. We turn real problems into products people can use.

Dream Invent Inspire, Inc. is a Delaware C corporation based in the Pittsburgh area (Cranberry Twp / Evans City PA). We invent and ship across hardware and software: growing systems that put food freedom in more hands, and a governed backend platform that helps teams put AI on structured data without guessing.
